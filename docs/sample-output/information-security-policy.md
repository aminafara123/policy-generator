## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes Northwind Health Labs's commitment to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the information and systems it relies on to operate in the B2B SaaS / health-tech sector. It is the top-level policy from which all other security policies and standards derive, and it supports Northwind Health Labs's obligations under ISO/IEC 27001:2022, SOC 2 Type II.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all information Northwind Health Labs creates, receives, or processes, including Customer PII, Protected Health Information (PHI), Payment card data (via a PCI-compliant processor), Source code and business financials, across all environments used to run the business, including AWS (eu-west-1, eu-central-1), Cloudflare, Google Workspace. It covers all Northwind Health Labs employees, contractors, interns, and third parties with access to company systems or data.

## 3. Information Security Objectives
- Protect customer, employee, and company data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and loss.
- Meet the applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations of a 85 employees organisation in B2B SaaS / health-tech.
- Maintain the availability of services that customers depend on.
- Continually improve the security posture through risk assessment and review.

## 4. Policy Statements
- Information security is managed through a risk-based approach aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022, SOC 2 Type II.
- Access to information is granted on a least-privilege, need-to-know basis.
- All personnel are responsible for protecting the information they handle and for reporting suspected incidents.
- Security requirements are considered in projects, system changes, and vendor relationships from the outset.
- Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, and may be reported to authorities where required by law.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities
- **Chief Executive Officer (CEO)** owns the overall risk posture and approves this policy.
- **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)** maintains the security programme, the supporting policies, and the risk register, and reports status to leadership.
- **Managers** ensure their teams understand and follow this policy.
- **All personnel** complete security awareness training and follow the policies that apply to their role.
- Security contact for questions and reports: the Security team (security@northwind.example).

## 6. Risk Management
Northwind Health Labs maintains a risk assessment process that identifies, analyses, and treats information security risks at least annually and when significant changes occur. Risk treatment decisions and residual risk are recorded and accepted by Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) or Chief Executive Officer (CEO).

## 7. Compliance and Exceptions
Compliance with this policy is mandatory. Any exception must be requested in writing, justified by business need, risk-assessed, time-bound, and approved by Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). Exceptions are recorded and reviewed on expiry.

## 8. Review
This policy is reviewed at least annually, and after any major change to the business, technology, or regulatory environment, by Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and approved by Chief Executive Officer (CEO).

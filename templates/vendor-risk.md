# {{company_name}} Vendor & Third-Party Risk Policy

> **Control mapping.** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A 5.19 (information security in supplier relationships), 5.20 (addressing security in supplier agreements), 5.21 (managing security in the ICT supply chain), 5.22 (monitoring & review of supplier services), 5.23 (security for cloud services) · SOC 2 CC9.2.

**Owner:** {{policy_owner}} · **Approver:** {{approver}} · **Classification:** Internal · **Applies to:** all {{company_name}} personnel who engage third parties, and the suppliers themselves.

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how {{company_name}} assesses and manages the security risk of vendors and third parties, so that suppliers who handle {{data_types}} or support critical services meet {{company_name}}'s security expectations under {{frameworks}}.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all third parties that process {{company_name}} data, access its systems, or provide services material to its operations, including {{cloud_providers}}, SaaS providers, subprocessors, and contractors.

## 3. Risk-Based Tiering
Vendors are tiered by the risk they present, based on the sensitivity of data they handle (for example {{data_types}}), their access to {{company_name}} systems, and their importance to service delivery. Higher-tier vendors receive more scrutiny before and during the relationship.

## 4. Due Diligence Before Engagement
- Before a vendor is engaged, {{company_name}} assesses its security posture proportionate to its tier (for example security questionnaires, SOC 2 or ISO 27001 reports, and penetration test summaries).
- Vendors handling {{data_types}} must demonstrate controls at least equivalent to {{company_name}}'s own.
- Findings are recorded and residual risk is accepted by {{policy_owner}} before onboarding.

## 5. Contracts
Agreements with in-scope vendors include security and confidentiality obligations, data protection and breach-notification requirements, the right to audit or request evidence, subprocessor controls, and secure return or deletion of {{company_name}} data at termination.

## 6. Ongoing Monitoring
- Higher-tier vendors are re-assessed at least annually, and when their service or risk changes materially.
- {{company_name}} tracks vendor security incidents and SOC 2 / ISO 27001 report renewals, and follows up on noted exceptions.
- A current inventory of in-scope vendors and their tiers is maintained.

## 7. Offboarding
When a vendor relationship ends, access is revoked, {{company_name}} data is returned or securely deleted with confirmation obtained, and the vendor is removed from the active inventory.

## 8. Roles and Responsibilities
- **Relationship owners** identify vendor risk and initiate assessments.
- **{{policy_owner}}** maintains this policy, the vendor inventory, and the assessment process, and accepts residual risk.

## 9. Review
This policy is reviewed at least annually by {{policy_owner}} and approved by {{approver}}.

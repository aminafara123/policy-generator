# {{company_name}} Acceptable Use Policy

> **Control mapping.** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A 5.10 (acceptable use of information and assets), 6.3 (awareness), 6.7 (remote working), 8.1 (user endpoint devices), 8.7 (protection against malware) · SOC 2 CC1.1, CC2.2, CC6.7.

**Owner:** {{policy_owner}} · **Approver:** {{approver}} · **Classification:** Internal · **Applies to:** all {{company_name}} personnel, contractors, and anyone using {{company_name}} systems.

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out how {{company_name}} information, devices, and systems may and may not be used, so that users at a {{company_size}} {{industry}} company protect company and customer data in their daily work.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all use of {{company_name}} systems, networks, email, and devices, and to the handling of {{data_types}}, whether the work is done in an office, remotely, or on {{cloud_providers}}.

## 3. Acceptable Use
- Company systems and data are provided for legitimate business purposes. Limited, reasonable personal use is permitted where it does not interfere with work, consume excessive resources, or breach this policy.
- Users protect their credentials, lock their screens when away, and keep company devices with them or secured.
- Users store company data only in approved systems and locations.

## 4. Unacceptable Use
- Sharing credentials, disabling security controls, or attempting to bypass access restrictions.
- Installing unauthorised software, or using unapproved cloud services ("shadow IT") to store or process {{data_types}}.
- Accessing, storing, or sending illegal, harassing, or infringing material.
- Connecting unauthorised devices to company networks or exfiltrating company data for non-business use.

## 5. Email, Messaging, and Internet
- Users treat unexpected links and attachments with caution and report suspected phishing to {{security_contact}}.
- Sensitive information, including {{data_types}}, is sent only through approved, protected channels.
- Users do not represent personal opinions as those of {{company_name}}.

## 6. Devices and Remote Work
- Company and approved personal devices used for work are kept patched, encrypted where supported, and protected with a screen lock and endpoint protection.
- Remote work follows {{company_name}}'s access and data-handling rules; public Wi-Fi is used only with the approved VPN or equivalent protection.

## 7. Monitoring and Privacy
{{company_name}} may monitor and log the use of its systems for security, legal, and operational purposes, to the extent permitted by law. Use of company systems implies consent to this monitoring.

## 8. Enforcement
Breaches of this policy may lead to withdrawal of access and disciplinary action up to and including termination, and may be reported to authorities where required.

## 9. Review
This policy is reviewed at least annually by {{policy_owner}} and approved by {{approver}}.

# {{company_name}} Information Security Policy

> **Control mapping.** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A 5.1 (policies for information security), 5.2 (roles & responsibilities), 5.3 (segregation of duties), 5.4 (management responsibilities), 6.3 (awareness) · SOC 2 CC1.1–CC1.5, CC2.2, CC5.3.

**Owner:** {{policy_owner}} · **Approver:** {{approver}} · **Classification:** Internal · **Applies to:** all {{company_name}} personnel, contractors, and systems.

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes {{company_name}}'s commitment to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the information and systems it relies on to operate in the {{industry}} sector. It is the top-level policy from which all other security policies and standards derive, and it supports {{company_name}}'s obligations under {{frameworks}}.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all information {{company_name}} creates, receives, or processes, including {{data_types}}, across all environments used to run the business, including {{cloud_providers}}. It covers all {{company_name}} employees, contractors, interns, and third parties with access to company systems or data.

## 3. Information Security Objectives
- Protect customer, employee, and company data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and loss.
- Meet the applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations of a {{company_size}} organisation in {{industry}}.
- Maintain the availability of services that customers depend on.
- Continually improve the security posture through risk assessment and review.

## 4. Policy Statements
- Information security is managed through a risk-based approach aligned with {{frameworks}}.
- Access to information is granted on a least-privilege, need-to-know basis.
- All personnel are responsible for protecting the information they handle and for reporting suspected incidents.
- Security requirements are considered in projects, system changes, and vendor relationships from the outset.
- Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, and may be reported to authorities where required by law.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities
- **{{approver}}** owns the overall risk posture and approves this policy.
- **{{policy_owner}}** maintains the security programme, the supporting policies, and the risk register, and reports status to leadership.
- **Managers** ensure their teams understand and follow this policy.
- **All personnel** complete security awareness training and follow the policies that apply to their role.
- Security contact for questions and reports: {{security_contact}}.

## 6. Risk Management
{{company_name}} maintains a risk assessment process that identifies, analyses, and treats information security risks at least annually and when significant changes occur. Risk treatment decisions and residual risk are recorded and accepted by {{policy_owner}} or {{approver}}.

## 7. Compliance and Exceptions
Compliance with this policy is mandatory. Any exception must be requested in writing, justified by business need, risk-assessed, time-bound, and approved by {{policy_owner}}. Exceptions are recorded and reviewed on expiry.

## 8. Review
This policy is reviewed at least annually, and after any major change to the business, technology, or regulatory environment, by {{policy_owner}} and approved by {{approver}}.
